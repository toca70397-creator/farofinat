Farofinat Promo Hub

Farofinat Promo Hub is a private tool used by the Farofinat music project to manage its own short videos. A video editor uploads finished clips, the channel owner reviews them, and approved clips are published to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account at a chosen time through the official YouTube Data API and TikTok Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. The editor uploads a finished video file.
  2. The owner reviews it, writes the title and hashtags, chooses who can see the TikTok post, and approves it.
  3. At the scheduled time the video is published to the owner's connected accounts. The owner can cancel before that.

The tool is used only by the account owner for their own accounts. It does not post on behalf of other users, does not read followers' data, and does not use any automation to inflate views, likes or followers.
