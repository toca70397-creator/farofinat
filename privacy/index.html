Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Farofinat Promo Hub (“the app”) is a private tool that the Farofinat project uses to publish its own videos to its own YouTube channel and TikTok account. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we access

  • YouTube (Google): with the owner's permission the app uploads videos to the owner's channel (youtube.upload) and reads basic channel information and the status of uploaded videos (youtube.readonly).
  • TikTok: with the owner's permission the app reads basic profile information (user.info.basic: display name, open ID) and publishes videos to the owner's account (video.publish).
  • Videos and texts uploaded by the project's editor, and titles/descriptions written by the owner.

How data is stored

The app runs on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are stored there in encrypted form and are used only to publish the owner's videos. Video files are kept only until they have been published and are deleted automatically a few days later. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Third-party services

Videos are sent only to YouTube and TikTok through their official APIs. Their own privacy policies apply: Google Privacy Policy, TikTok Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can disconnect YouTube or TikTok in the app at any time, or revoke access in Google account permissions and in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions). After disconnecting, the stored tokens are deleted.
